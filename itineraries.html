Choosing where to go

For a shorter break, look first at nearby Italian cities such as Milan, Venice, and Rome. For destinations like Dublin, Malta, Croatia, Greece, or Ibiza, compare flight and transfer times and consider a longer break if needed.

Before you book: This is an educational travel guide, not a booking service. Check current schedules, prices, recent accommodation reviews, entry requirements, and attraction hours with official providers.
