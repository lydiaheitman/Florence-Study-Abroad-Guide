How to compare your full weekend budget

Add up round-trip transportation, local transfers, lodging, city taxes, meals, attraction tickets, and an emergency cushion. A cheap flight may cost more after baggage and airport transfers.

Check transport first

Train or bus can be easier for nearby Italian cities; flights may be more practical for farther destinations. Compare door-to-door travel time.
