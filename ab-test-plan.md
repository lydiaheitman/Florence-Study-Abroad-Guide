# A/B Test Plan – Florence Study Abroad Guide

## 1. Page and Variable

I am testing the homepage of my Florence Study Abroad Guide website. I want to see whether changing the homepage color scheme affects how likely students are to explore my website.

## 2. Version A and Version B

- **Version A (Control):** My original homepage with a burgundy color scheme.
- **Version B (Treatment):** The same homepage with an orange color scheme.

All other elements, including the pictures, text, buttons, and layout, will stay the same.

## 3. Hypothesis

I predict that the orange homepage will encourage more students to explore my website. The burgundy color may be perceived as more feminine by some visitors, while orange may feel more gender neutral and welcoming to a wider range of students.

## 4. AI Model and Visitor Persona

- **App:** ChatGPT
- **Model:** GPT-6
- **Plan:** Plus
- **Date:** November 2026
- **Settings:** GPT-6, ChatGPT Plus. Exact thinking mode and web search settings will be recorded before testing.

The AI visitors will represent college students studying abroad in Florence, Italy, who are interested in affordable weekend trips around Europe.

### Version A Prompt – Burgundy Homepage

"You are a college student studying abroad in Florence, Italy, looking for affordable weekend trips around Europe. Look at the attached homepage screenshot. Based only on this page, would you click the 'Explore Destinations' button to learn more about weekend travel options? Answer Yes or No and briefly explain why."

For Version A, I will attach a screenshot of my original homepage with the burgundy color scheme.

### Version B Prompt – Orange Homepage

"You are a college student studying abroad in Florence, Italy, looking for affordable weekend trips around Europe. Look at the attached homepage screenshot. Based only on this page, would you click the 'Explore Destinations' button to learn more about weekend travel options? Answer Yes or No and briefly explain why."

For Version B, I will attach a screenshot of the same homepage with the orange color scheme.

I will use the exact same prompt for both versions and only change the homepage screenshot. This will allow me to test whether the color scheme affects the AI visitors' responses.

## 5. Random Assignment

I will use Excel to randomly assign 100 AI visitors to Version A or Version B. I will number the visitors from 1 to 100 and use Excel's RAND function to generate random numbers. I will sort the numbers and assign the first 50 visitors to Version A and the remaining 50 to Version B. This will help make sure the groups are randomly assigned.

I will use a new ChatGPT conversation for each trial and keep the AI model, prompt, and settings consistent.

## 6. Outcome Measurement

I will measure the percentage of AI visitors who say they would click the Explore Destinations button. I will record each response as Yes or No in Excel and compare the percentage of Yes responses for Version A and Version B.

These responses will represent simulated visitor intentions rather than actual website clicks.

## 7. Sample Size, Dates, and Analysis

I plan to complete 100 AI visitor trials, with 50 visitors assigned to each version. I will use the same AI model and visitor instructions for both versions and record the results in Excel.

I will complete all 100 trials before analyzing the results and will not stop early based on the responses.

I will compare the percentage of Yes responses between the two versions to determine which homepage color performs better. I will also consider whether the difference could be due to random variation.
